# Learner Pathways

## Routing table

| Learner | Instructional emphasis | Suitable evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K-5 | Digital citizenship, privacy, passwords, safe communication, trusted-adult help | Sort, explain, demonstrate a safe choice, create a safety plan |
| Grades 6-8 | Systems, networks, data protection, threat awareness, ethical defensive thinking | Annotated diagram, evidence table, tabletop decision, simple configuration check |
| Grades 9-12 | Defensive operations, scripting foundations, risk, incident analysis, career exploration | Log findings, hardening checklist, incident timeline, short script, briefing |
| College | Technical foundations, analysis, design tradeoffs, team operations | Lab artifact, analysis report, configuration, detection rule, presentation |
| Career changer | Job tasks, portfolio evidence, workplace scenarios, certification support | Work sample, ticket, incident note, risk recommendation, practical demonstration |
| Professional | Role-specific decisions, advanced practice, organizational constraints | Operational plan, technical artifact, peer briefing, after-action review |
| Educator | Content confidence, facilitation, troubleshooting, assessment, safe lab management | Teach-back, lesson adaptation, facilitation plan, learner-work calibration |

## Experience levels

- **First exposure:** define vocabulary in context; model every tool interaction; minimize setup.
- **Beginner:** provide a worked example and checkpoints; assess one primary technique.
- **Intermediate:** require independent selection of techniques and explanation of tradeoffs.
- **Advanced:** introduce ambiguity, incomplete evidence, constraints, and defensible judgment.

Do not infer technical proficiency from age, degree, job title, disability, language background, or
prior access to technology.

## Support profiles

Use Guided, Standard, and Challenge when multiple versions are requested. Never print labels such
as "below level," "remedial," or "advanced student" on learner materials.

Provide alternatives for:

- No administrator privileges.
- No command-line access.
- Low bandwidth or no internet.
- Screen reader or keyboard-only navigation.
- Shared devices.
- Limited class time.
- Language and vocabulary support.
